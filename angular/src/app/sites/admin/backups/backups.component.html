Every database in one go — accounts, translations and game content together, because restoring one afternoon's content against another afternoon's accounts is the mess a backup is meant to prevent. Files stay on the server; use Download in a backup to get one somewhere else. Nothing here restores anything: that is a deliberate act for a shell prompt, with the site stopped.

Live is what a database takes up on disk right now — rows, every index, and space Postgres has reclaimed but not handed back, which is why an empty one is still several megabytes. A dump holds only the rows, compressed, and rebuilds the indexes on restore. A dump coming out several times smaller is normal and is not a sign anything was left out; the row counts inside a backup are what confirm that.
